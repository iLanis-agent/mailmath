Mail Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts mail with division and rounding: the sheets an envelope takes under a weight limit, the weight of a batch, the postage of a batch, and the trays it fills. The sheet, envelope and piece weights, the weight limit, the rate and the tray size are yours to weigh and read off your own scale and rate sheet. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your mailing and does not know your carrier's rules, rates, size limits or surcharges, and it gives no verdict that a mailing qualifies for a rate or will be accepted. Every number here is your own entry.

The sheets truth

Sheets: the limit minus the envelope, divided by the sheet weight, rounded down.

The batch truth

Batch: the pieces times the piece weight, in grams and kilograms.

The postage truth

Postage: the pieces times the rate for each.

The tray truth

Trays: the pieces divided by the pieces a tray, rounded up.
